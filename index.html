The scan

It starts with a scan. Here's the engine behind it.

The placement scan is not written by hand. Lensmaker builds it, from public information only, by running your company through the same pipeline it runs in production every week. A cold, outside-in market intelligence snapshot: where you sit, the angle you can own, where you're exposed, and the one move worth making now.

That is the product introducing itself. Now picture it warm: tuned to your roadmap, your voice, and the objection that keeps stalling your deals. The snapshot starts moving: living market intelligence that signals your next move, week after week.

One engine, tuned to you

Same engine for every client. The lens is yours alone.

Your context is the difference between market noise and a signal you can act on. It runs on your Blend: a tested, tunable mix of context that becomes the lens the engine looks through, built to sharpen your positioning and your market responses.

01 · Your context

The Blend

Eight parts only you can supply: thesis, profile, priorities, positioning angle, competitive set, sources, filters, voice. Blended to your taste, and no one else's.

02 · The work

The agents

A pipeline of agents gathers, filters, interprets, and drafts between sessions. About an hour a week from you while we tune the Blend. Then it runs.

03 · The output

The read

Not what happened. What it means for you, and what to do about it, written in your voice and ready to use.

Govern-check

Every claim is grounded to a cited source before it reaches you, and a separate auditor agent, fresh eyes and no stake in the output, checks the work. Competitive reads come leashed, marked to verify before you take them into a room. Honest by design.

The thread through time

A report is a photograph. This is time-lapse.

Because it watches every week, you don't just see the market. You see the market respond to you: the campaign that landed, the launch that moved a competitor, the tradeshow that shifted the conversation.

A one-shot scan tells you where you stand today. Run week over week, the same engine weaves a thread through time, and that thread is where the real intelligence lives.
